01 Poetry

Goodbye

I'm highly aware that one day
I will take my last breath,
but losing you
is something I would never wish.

I know our ways are losing each other,
but that has never been my wish.
I drop tears every time I think about it.

You are the joy I first met in my life.
You brought a happiness
I had never experienced.

I'm useless—completely.
Sometimes I wish I had never met you.
You left the most regrettable scar on my life.

Girl, tell me,
how am I going to survive?
In fact, I doubt
if I will even be alive.

I'm hopeless.
Speechless.
Insane.

In pain.

It started simple.
Look at us now—
stuck in the shackles
of our secret love story.

I never thought
I would tell you this to your face,

but goodbye.

02 Poetry

The Fruit

You may be dismayed by the beauty
until you taste the worst fruit.

You can't appreciate the fruit
while hurting its roots.
The aesthetic of it
is never found in looks alone.

Take time to hold it tight
and taste its sweetness.
Even before your tongue feels it,
it will already be felt by your lips.

Open your eyes.
Its beauty, aroma, and taste
might be an ambush
set by tricksters.

Indeed,
if it is not well watered,
it might fade.

If it is not held tightly,
it may fly away.

The imagery of words
makes no actions in reality's space.

Trust is a simple word,
yet it is difficult
to receive and believe.

We seize the moment when it is given,
but you need to be precise—
it might be the wrong moment.

I'm too peniaphobic
to find out
what happens if this doesn't work out.

03 Poetry

Elixir of Life

You are the strings
of my love guitar,

so firmly attached
that the melody they produce
makes my smile grow wider.

You plucked me
from the shackles of intruders.

It was then.

Now, my thoughts are enslaved
by your beauty.

I don't think
I can even fantasize
a life with others.

The memories we created
almost faded away
because of my envy.

Being the guardian angel
you claimed what was yours
and put wrong thoughts on pause.

The remote control's button is lost.

You are the candlelight
that keeps me ablaze,

the oxygen
that keeps me alive,

the pillar
that gives me strength,

the support
that makes me survive.

Girl,
you are my elixir of life,

making me drunk
with your sweetness
while healing
my love disease.

We are obligated
to make this love blossom,

as we let it grow
within our eyes—

mm... that's awesome.

For that makes me your tason,
as you are my bosom.
